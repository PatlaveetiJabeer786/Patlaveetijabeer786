### 🔴 Project 01 — Blinkit Sales & Performance Analysis
**`SQL` `Python` `Power BI` `Excel`** | 

> **Business Problem:** 8,500+ SKUs across outlet tiers with no data on which inventory earns vs. drains working capital.

- ✅ Uncovered **$472K revenue underinvestment gap** in Tier 3 outlets (39.3% of revenue on lowest spend)
- ✅ Identified **Low Fat segment: ₹28.4L untargeted revenue** — zero dedicated SKU coverage
- ✅ Found top 312 SKUs of 1,248 drive **79.6% of revenue** — 75% catalog is deadweight
- 🎯 **Decision:** Reallocate ₹12–15L from over-invested Tier 1 to Tier 3 + cut bottom 25% SKUs in 30 days

### 🔴 Project 02 — Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
**`SQL` `Python` `Power BI`** | 

> **Business Problem:** Marketing budget spread equally across all segments with no ROI evidence per segment.

- ✅ Proved promo users spent **18–22% more per order** across 3,900+ transactions

### 🔴 Project 03 — Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis
**`Python`**

> **Business Problem:** 37% of 119,390 confirmed bookings cancelled with zero deposit obligation.

- ✅ Found **61–90 day advance bookings cancel at 2× rate** of last-minute bookings
- ✅ City hotels: **41.7% cancel rate** vs Resort: 27.8% — two different problems
- ✅ July–August demand **3× baseline** — pricing not capturing willingness-to-pay
- 🎯 **Decision:** Enforce 30% non-refundable deposit for all 60+ day city hotel bookings
